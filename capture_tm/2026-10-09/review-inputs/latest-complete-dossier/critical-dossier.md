## Problem and candidate hypotheses

Frozen problem: under identical causal previews, legal physical actions, budget and fixed target, does exposure adaptation retain identifiable value after the renderer sufficiently uses available observations?

Candidate claims for examination, not asserted established:
C1. The remaining value of exposure choice depends on backend compensation/information scope.
C2. Matched pixel marginals with changed spatial dependence can alter useful capture choices identifiable from causal previews.
C3. Any resulting deployable benefit survives sufficient reconstruction and independent-backend evaluation and is not already explained by simple brightness/local rules.

The new diagnostic action catalog is [.125,.25,.5,1,2,4,8]. Toy latent marginals are a uniform (.05,.30) and b uniform .8..1.8, mixed independent/copula training prior. The fixed target is sqrt(l/(l+.5)). Three independent noisy previews at .25 are supplied to policies; future capture follows the declared Poisson/read/clip/ADC chain. The history-reference protocol contrasts renderers conditioned on future capture only or on all H plus future capture and independently freezes development selectors for a full crossover. N1 changes true dependence while the renderer's training prior stays fixed.

The DNG relative diagnostic is a separate protocol using bounded processed green content as a surrogate source, fixed target, identical relative action/noise assumptions, camera-specific train-only point priors and inverse/local-Wiener controls. Source provenance, background-group split and limitations are in raw audit/protocol files. This does not reproduce original S24 neural training or provide true multi-exposure recaptures.
